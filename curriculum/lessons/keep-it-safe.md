# Keep it safe and running

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Whoever it's for.** Never give any AI your wallet recovery phrase or private keys. Try everything with test items first, and get a second person to check anything that holds other people's items or money.
{% endhint %}

## For what you're building

```by-answer
answer: audience
default: me
variants:
  me: |-
    - **Cost:** Anything that bills by use, like AI account keys or hosting, can surprise you. Set a spending limit first.
    - **Security:** Keep passwords and AI account keys out of your code and out of the chat.
    - **Upkeep:** When the game updates, your tool may need a fix. Ask your AI to check what changed.
  friends: |-
    - **Cost:** Free hosting has limits. Find out what happens if you go over before you share the link.
    - **Security:** Other people use it now. Keep passwords and account keys out of the code and out of the chat.
    - **Upkeep:** Someone has to fix it when the game updates. That's you, and your AI.
  tribe: |-
    - **Cost:** More use means more cost. Set alerts as well as limits.
    - **Security:** Check every change on a private test link before it goes live, and keep account keys somewhere safe.
    - **Upkeep:** People notice when it breaks. Keep a way back to the last version that worked.
  everyone: |-
    - **Cost:** Traffic can spike overnight. Know your worst-case bill before launch.
    - **Security:** Expect people to poke at it. Ask your AI to review it for security before it goes live.
    - **Upkeep:** Updates, outages, questions from players: plan for keeping it running, not just building it.
```

## Keep a safe copy

GitHub keeps a copy of your code online. Changes are tried on a branch, a separate copy away from the version that works. Commits are save points. When a change works, it's merged into the working version. If something breaks, your working version is untouched. Your AI does the GitHub part; you just ask.

```by-answer

variants:
  friends: 'Anything other people use: check it on a private test link before it goes live.'
  tribe: 'Anything other people use: check it on a private test link before it goes live.'
  everyone: 'Anything other people use: check it on a private test link before it goes live.'
```
