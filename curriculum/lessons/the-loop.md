# The loop

1. **Describe** (You): Say what you want and tell it where to look. You can ramble; it sorts out the order. Ask it to question you and plan before it builds.
2. **Plan** (AI, then you): Asked to, it reads the sources, asks you questions, then writes a short plan. You approve it. Left alone, it often guesses instead.
3. **Build** (AI): Ask for the smallest piece that works first. Left alone, many AIs build everything at once.
4. **Prove** (AI, then you): It shows you it working: a screenshot, a private test link, or a test result. You look.
5. **Save** (AI): It saves a [checkpoint](glossary:checkpoint), a save point it can go back to, and notes what was decided, when you ask.

**Then round again with the next piece.**

{% hint style="info" %}
Most AIs only plan, work in small steps and save when you ask. Put the asking in your ground rules once, and it happens every time. How well it goes also depends on which AI you use.
{% endhint %}

Doing it this way is sometimes called [agentic engineering](glossary:agentic-engineering). Skipping the plan and the proof is often called [vibe coding](glossary:vibe-coding): great for a quick experiment, risky for anything people rely on.
