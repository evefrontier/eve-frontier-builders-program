# You never write the code

- **You, the project manager.** Say what you want. Look at what comes back. Decide what's next.
- **AI agents, your team.** Plan it, write the code, test it, fix it, and write down what they did.
- **Your tool, the result.** Something that works, built a piece at a time.

Your skills are describing what you want, looking at what comes back, and saying what's wrong.

You know the game; it knows the code. You say what matters in game, it says what's possible in code, and you get there together.

{% hint style="info" %}
An [AI agent](glossary:ai-agent) is an AI that does the work, not just chats about it: it can write files, run them and check the result. A chat app on its own is the first step towards one.
{% endhint %}
